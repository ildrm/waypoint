# Specialist role charters

Owner: R01. Reviewers: R31, R34. Status: assigned perspectives, 2026-10-29. Source: authorized brief. Related: R01–34 and all deliverables. Open decisions: DEC-01–10. Practice: [quality profile](skills/quality-documentation.md).

These are accountability perspectives, not a34-person hiring plan. Drafts were assigned to actual documentation agents by domain. Role-specific judgments inside those drafts are simulated specialist perspectives; none is a human or certified specialist approval. R34's assurance perspective is not organizational independence.

All roles receive the brief, relevant answers and linked assumptions; produce the named deliverable and acceptance evidence; escalate conflicting scope or unsupported claims to the accountable owner. Only an authorized human owner can approve production/commercial/legal decisions.

Each artifact has exactly one accountable owner in raci.csv; reviewers supply consulted challenge. R31 maintains the record, not authority over another role's commercial or legal decisions. R33 is conditional because AI is deferred. R20 supplies mobile recommendation expertise even though the platform's first public surface is web.

Domain activation: commerce/payment answers activate commerce/payments review (refunds, settlement, tax/provider responsibility); regulated financial/health/education activities activate qualified jurisdiction/domain review before promises; GIS/device/industrial integrations activate capability and safety review; educational proficiency placement requires curriculum owner, evidence-based level definitions, validated assessment and qualified language/domain review. Triggered specialists deliver constraints, acceptance criteria and unresolved issues; no automatic full-time hiring assumption.
