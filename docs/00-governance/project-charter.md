# Project charter

Owner: R01. Reviewers: R02, R31, R34. Status: proposed specification, 2026-10-29; no human approval.
Sources: [authorized brief](sources/documentation-super-mega-prompt.md), [inventory](source-inventory.md). Related IDs: NEED-01–08. Open decisions: [decision log](assumptions-and-open-questions.md). Practice: [product-requirements](skills/product-requirements.md).

The platform turns answers into explainable next actions, software scope, solution alternatives and preliminary estimates. It serves founders, purchasers of software, advisers and the operator's content/commercial staff. Success means users can see why each material recommendation follows from their answers and what remains uncertain.

This assignment delivers documentation only. Application implementation, dependency installation, migrations, deployment, purchases and third-party contact are excluded. Existing source documents remain preserved. The PHP constraint applies to this platform; evaluated customer projects may use any justified technology.

Confirmed constraints: exactly fourteen production languages, independent adaptive flows sharing one engine, configurable commercial catalogs, typed shared/flow settings, historical reproducibility, polished responsive accessible design and RTL/LTR support. Fourteen language identities are confirmed in the owner clarification. Source locale, regional/script variants, estimate currency and operating jurisdiction remain open. Platform use is free and no payments are collected.

Proposed first release includes startup, software discovery, service matching, answer review/results, scenario comparison and the supporting admin/session/support workflows. Educational proficiency placement is conditional; no supplied curriculum exists. Optional narrative AI is deferred and cannot price or determine authoritative conclusions.

Acceptance is a complete proposed design with traceability, substantive workflow banks, worked examples, static mockups, source references and recorded documentation checks. Delivery does not imply production readiness, stakeholder sign-off, calibrated estimates or linguistic approval. R01 owns product decisions, R31 maintains the documentation, R34 challenges unsupported claims. Changes require impact analysis across requirements, questions, rules, outputs, prices, translations and acceptance records.
