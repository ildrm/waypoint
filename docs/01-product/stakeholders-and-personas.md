# Stakeholders and persona hypotheses

Owner: R07. Reviewers: R01, R14. Status: proposed specification, 2026-10-29; no human approval.
Sources: [brief](../00-governance/sources/documentation-super-mega-prompt.md), [source inventory](../00-governance/source-inventory.md). Assumptions/open decisions: [DEC-01–10](../00-governance/assumptions-and-open-questions.md). Related IDs: NEED-01–08. Practice: [experience-research](../00-governance/skills/experience-research.md).

These are hypotheses to recruit against, not completed research.

| Persona | Task/context | Friction and design response | Validation task |
|---|---|---|---|
| First-time founder | Explore venture on phone; limited evidence | Plain wording; unknown available; explain evidence levels | Distinguish interest from payment and choose next experiment |
| Software sponsor | Compare a site/app plan under budget/date constraints | Progressive domain branches; units/examples; review feature matrix | Explain why offline/device needs change solution |
| Technical adviser | Audit/refine scope with sponsor | Evidence IDs, alternatives and unresolved integrations | Trace estimate to answers and challenge assumptions |
| Content author | Change a flow safely | Stable IDs, impact previews and simulator | Edit upstream branch and inspect dependent invalidation |
| Language reviewer | Review edited business copy | Separate admin/content locale, source hash and stale status | Prevent stale critical text publication |
| Commercial approver | Maintain rates with effective dates | Exact amounts, scope/overrides, sample diff | Approve change without repricing history |
| Support operator | Help restore access/report export | Permissioned diagnostics and audit; no impersonated answer changes | Resolve export failure without viewing unrelated project |

Stakeholders include product sponsor, end users, content/commercial owners, delivery partners, security/privacy/operations and language specialists. Decision rights are in RACI. Recruitment should include novice/expert, mobile/desktop, assistive-technology and RTL/LTR participants; actual languages depend on DEC-01. Avoid collecting sensitive venture details just to qualify a research participant.
