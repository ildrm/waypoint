# ADR-005: Free platform, informational project prices

Owner: R01. Reviewers: R29, R23. Status: proposed documentation, 2026-10-29; no human approval.
Sources: [authorized brief](../../00-governance/sources/documentation-super-mega-prompt.md) and user clarification (14 named languages; free use; no platform payments). Related IDs: FR-29, AC-16. Open decisions: [register](../../00-governance/assumptions-and-open-questions.md). Practice: [architecture-data-api](../../00-governance/skills/architecture-data-api.md).

Owner-confirmed constraint: users use platform free of charge and no platform payments occur. Proposal implementation boundary: no checkout, billing, payment capture, invoices, paid entitlements or paywall. Rate catalogs calculate informational customer-project estimates only. Customer-project commerce/payment requirements remain elicited when applicable.

Rejected: interpreting a project estimate as a platform fee or adding subscription billing on organization support. Abuse limits/queues are allowed to protect free service without monetization. Operating funding and budget remain owner responsibilities. Constraint confirmed; engineering realization proposed/unimplemented.
