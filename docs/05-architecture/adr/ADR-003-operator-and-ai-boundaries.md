# ADR-003: Operator and AI boundaries

Owner: R01. Reviewers: R17, R23, R24, R33. Status: proposed. Source: prompt distinctions. Related: AC-07, AC-09. Open: organizations/AI business need.

Proposal: baseline has one operator and project ownership. If separate customer organizations are required, reopen identity, permissions, organizational constraints and support isolation; free/no-payment requirement remains before implementation. Customer-project tenancy remains independent evidence.

AI is off initially. Structured rules own scope, price, schedule and allocations. A future narrative assistant can explain approved facts only after provider/data review, evaluation, output checks and deterministic fallback. It cannot invent requirements or set prices.

Alternatives: premature tenancy adds ambiguous access expectations; unconditional AI adds privacy/cost/non-determinism without evidence. Revisit with owner need and new ADR. Acceptance: narrative edits cannot change calculations; customer tenancy answers cannot change platform authorization.
