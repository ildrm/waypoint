Separate platform operator funding from customer-project TCO. Free platform users pay nothing; operator hosting, email, backups, staff and translation costs still need a funded owner budget. No subscription/payment implementation is implied.

For evaluated projects list compute/database/storage/backups/egress/CDN, email/SMS, realtime/maps/search, inference if selected, monitoring, security/maintenance, content/localization, domain/licenses and app-store accounts where applicable. Each line has unit, usage, period, currency, region, inclusion, source/access date and overage assumption. Unknown vendor prices remain TBD/manual review; do not quote guessed market prices.
